# Delete your Guangel account and data

**App:** Guangel (Android, Google Play) · **Developer:** Hayde117

You can delete your Guangel account and all its data at any time, from the app or by email.

## In the app

1. Open Guangel and sign in.
2. Open the menu (top left), then **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and enter your password to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away. If an SOS is running, stop it first.

## Without the app

Email **haydelawal@gmail.com** from the email address you use for Guangel, with the subject "Delete my Guangel account". We will confirm the account is yours and delete it within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account (email address and password).
- Your trusted contacts, groups and custom messages.
- All alerts, with their locations, routes, audio recordings and links. Links you shared stop working.
- Your settings and checklist progress.
- The data Guangel stored on your phone (when you delete from the app).

## What is kept, and for how long

- Google's encrypted backups may hold copies for up to 30 more days before they are overwritten.
- For links you shared, we keep only a one-way hash of the link's code and the time it was removed, for up to 180 days, so the page can say "This link is no longer available". It contains nothing about you, your contacts or your location.
- Crash reports and analytics are not linked to your account, and expire on their own schedule (crash reports up to 90 days, analytics up to 14 months).

Uninstalling the app does **not** delete your account. Delete it first if you want everything removed.

To delete only some data, such as single alerts, use **History** in the app.

Full details: [Guangel Privacy Policy](https://github.com/Haydelawal/privacy_policy/blob/main/privacy_policy_guangel_app.md).
