Privacy Policy
Effective Date: Monday, July 15, 2025

Introduction
Thank you for using Spam App. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Spam App handles your information. Spam App is designed to work entirely offline using on-device machine learning. It does not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

Data Access and Use
Spam App uses a lightweight machine learning model (TensorFlow Lite) to analyze your input messages and determine if they are spam.
The analysis happens entirely on your device.
No internet connection is required.
No messages or results are sent to any external server or third party.
You are not required to log in or create an account.

Local Data and Storage
Spam App allows you to view and search previous analysis results.
These results are stored locally on your device using an internal database (Room).
Uninstalling the app deletes all stored data, as no data is tied to your identity or synced to any cloud/server.
The data is only accessible to you and remains private on your device.

Information We Collect
We do not collect any personal or non-personal information.
No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment details are collected.
No usage data, analytics, or crash reports are collected or transmitted.
No data is shared with any third-party service.

Data Sharing
We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data. All operations and results remain strictly on your device.

Security
While no data is collected or transmitted externally, we follow standard best practices to keep your local data secure on your device. However, please remember that uninstalling the app will delete all your stored results.

Children’s Privacy
Spam App does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 18. The app is suitable for users of all ages, but we recommend parental guidance for minors.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be clearly reflected within the app or through an app update.
