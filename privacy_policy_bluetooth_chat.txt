Privacy Policy
Effective Date: Monday, July 15, 2025

Introduction
Thank you for using Bluetooth Chat. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Bluetooth Chat handles your information. The app is designed to work entirely offline via Bluetooth and does not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

Information We Collect
Bluetooth Chat does not collect any personal or non-personal information.

No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or device identifiers are collected.

No usage data, location data, or analytics are tracked.

No internet connectivity is required or used.

How We Use Your Information
Since Bluetooth Chat does not collect any data, there is no information to use, share, or store. All messages are exchanged directly between devices via Bluetooth and are not stored by the app.

Data Sharing
We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any data at all. Your messages are transmitted only between devices using a secure Bluetooth connection and are not sent over the internet or to any server.

Security
Although we do not store or process personal data, we strive to provide a secure experience by using standard Bluetooth communication protocols. However, it's important to note that any message you send via Bluetooth is visible to the recipient device.

Children's Privacy
Bluetooth Chat does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 18. The app can be used by individuals of all ages, but we recommend parental supervision for minors.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the app or available via an update.
