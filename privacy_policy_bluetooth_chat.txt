Privacy Policy
Effective Date: Monday, July 15, 2025

Introduction
Thank you for using Bluetooth Chat. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Bluetooth Chat handles your information. The app is designed to work entirely offline via Bluetooth and does not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

Why Location Permission Is Required
Bluetooth Chat uses location permission solely because it is required by the Android operating system to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices.
We do not access, collect, or track your location data in any form.

This permission is necessary for the core functionality of the app — enabling Bluetooth discovery and communication between nearby devices.

Information We Collect
Bluetooth Chat does not collect any personal or non-personal information.

No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or device identifiers are collected.

No location data, usage data, or analytics are tracked or shared for any other reasons apart from solely scanning, discovering and connecting to other bluetooth devices.

No internet connectivity is required or used.

All Bluetooth communication happens directly between devices.

How We Use Your Information
Since Bluetooth Chat does not collect any data, there is no information to use, share, or store.
All messages are exchanged directly between devices via Bluetooth and are not stored by the app or sent to any server.

Data Sharing
We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data.
Your messages are transmitted only between devices using a secure Bluetooth connection — not over the internet.

Security
While we do not store or process any personal data, we follow standard security practices for Bluetooth communication.
However, keep in mind that messages sent over Bluetooth can be seen by the recipient device.

Children's Privacy
Bluetooth Chat does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 18.
The app is suitable for users of all ages, but we recommend parental supervision for minors.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected in the app or made available through an update.
